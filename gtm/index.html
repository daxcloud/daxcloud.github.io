How it runs

Eight to twelve weeks. Then you keep it.

The scope is set in writing at the end of week one. Nothing is sent without a grant you approve, and every document the engine produces lands on your drive.

Install

Immersion in what you sell and how you win. The engine lands on your stack, deliverability audited, targets researched and reviewed before anyone is touched.

Live sends ramp under grants you approve. Replies route to humans the minute they land. A weekly readout in plain numbers: delivered, opened, clicked, replied, booked.
